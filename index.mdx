# Welcome to uses.tech

uses.tech is a community-curated directory of `/uses` pages — a place where developers share the exact hardware, software, editors, tools, and configurations that power their work.

Browse thousands of developer setups, filter by technology stack, country, or device, and get inspired to build your own `/uses` page.

## How it works

uses.tech is an open-source project on GitHub. The entire directory lives in a single `data.js` file. Anyone can add their `/uses` page by submitting a pull request.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your /uses page">
    Build a page on your personal website at a URL ending in `/uses`, `/using`, `/setup`, or `/environment`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fork the repository">
    Fork [wesbos/awesome-uses](https://github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses) on GitHub and add your entry to `src/data.js`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a pull request">
    Open a PR with your name as the title. Automated validation checks your entry before it's merged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the directory">
    Once merged, your page appears in the live directory at [uses.tech](https://uses.tech) — browsable by tag, country, and device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

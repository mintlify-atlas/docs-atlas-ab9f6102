---

uses.tech is a community-maintained directory. Anyone with a `/uses` page can contribute by adding their entry directly to `src/data.js` in the [wesbos/awesome-uses](https://github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses) repository.

Every entry is validated automatically when you open a pull request. A GitHub Action checks your data against the Joi schema and verifies that your URL is reachable before a maintainer reviews your PR.

<Note>
  Read the [PR guidelines](/contributing/pr-guidelines) before submitting. Incorrectly titled PRs are the most common reason for delays.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fork the repository">
    Fork [wesbos/awesome-uses](https://github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses) on GitHub to create your own copy of the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your entry to src/data.js">
    Open `src/data.js` and insert your entry object somewhere in the middle of the array — not at the end. Follow the exact format of existing entries and make sure your URL points to a `/uses` page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a pull request">
    Push your branch and open a PR against the `master` branch. Use the correct title format: `Add Your Name` or `Add @yourhandle`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for automated validation">
    A GitHub Action runs automatically to validate your entry against the schema and check that your URL responds. If anything fails, the action leaves a comment explaining the issue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---
title: "Adding yourself to the directory"
description: "A step-by-step guide to forking the repo, adding your entry to src/data.js, and opening a pull request."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fork wesbos/awesome-uses on GitHub">
    Go to [github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses](https://github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses) and click **Fork**. This creates a copy of the repository under your GitHub account where you can make changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your entry to src/data.js">
    Open `src/data.js` in your forked repository. The file exports a plain JavaScript array of person objects. Add your entry anywhere in the middle of the array — the exact position doesn't matter as long as it's not at the very end.

    Here is the format to follow, taken from a real entry in the file:

    ```js
    {
      name: 'Phuc Bui',
      description:
        'Creative Frontend Engineer. I create things for designers and developers, usually open source.',
      url: 'https://phucbm.com/uses',
      emoji: '👽',
      country: '🇻🇳',
      computer: 'apple',
      phone: 'iphone',
      twitter: '@phucbm_',
      tags: [
        'Fullstack',
        'Frontend',
        'Web Animation',
        'GSAP',
        'Typescript',
        'React',
        'NextJS',
        'UI',
        'Freelance',
        'CSS',
        'JavaScript',
      ],
    },
    ```

    Replace each field with your own information. Only `name`, `description`, `url`, and `country` are required. All other fields are optional.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Don't add yourself to the end of the array">
    Insert your entry somewhere random in the middle of `src/data.js`, not at the bottom. This keeps the directory from becoming ordered by submission date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make sure your URL links to a /uses page">
    Your `url` must point to a page that describes your setup — not just your homepage. The validation schema enforces this: the URL must contain one of `use`, `uses`, `using`, `setup`, or `environment`, or it must be a GitHub Gist URL.

    <Warning>
      URLs that don't match the required pattern will fail automated validation and block your PR from merging. Double-check your link before submitting.
    </Warning>

    Valid examples:
    - `https://example.com/uses`
    - `https://example.com/my-setup`
    - `https://example.com/using`
    - `https://gist.github.com/username/abc123`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a PR with the correct title format">
    Push your branch to your fork and open a pull request against the `master` branch of `wesbos/awesome-uses`. Make sure the PR title follows the required format — see [PR guidelines](/contributing/pr-guidelines) for valid and invalid examples.

    <Tip>
      Include diverse tags in your entry to help people find your page when they filter the directory by technology or interest.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

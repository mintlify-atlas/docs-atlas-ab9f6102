---
title: "Data schema"
description: "Reference for every field in the Person object that makes up an entry in src/data.js."
---

Every entry in `src/data.js` must conform to the Joi schema defined in [`scripts/utils.js`](https://github.com/wesbos/awesome-uses/blob/master/scripts/utils.js). A GitHub Action validates every PR against this schema automatically.

## Fields

<ParamField path="body.name" type="string" required>
  Your display name as it will appear in the directory.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.description" type="string" required>
  A short tagline describing who you are or what you do. Shown beneath your name in the directory listing.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.url" type="string" required>
  The full URL to your `/uses` page. Must be a valid URI and must contain one of `use`, `uses`, `using`, `setup`, or `environment` in the path, or be a `gist.github.com` URL.

  <Warning>
    Linking to your homepage instead of your `/uses` page will cause validation to fail. The URL pattern is enforced by the schema.
  </Warning>
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.country" type="string" required>
  A flag emoji representing your country (e.g. `🇺🇸`, `🇩🇪`, `🇧🇷`). Must be a valid flag emoji from the list of supported values in `scripts/flags.js`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.twitter" type="string">
  Your Twitter/X username. Must match the pattern `@handle` where the handle is 1–15 word characters. The leading `@` is optional but conventional.

  Example: `@wesbos`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.mastodon" type="string">
  Your Mastodon username in the format `@handle@server.tld`.

  Example: `@kineticdiplomacy@infosec.exchange`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.bluesky" type="string">
  Your Bluesky handle. Do not include the leading `@`. Must match the format `username.bsky.social` or a custom domain handle like `user.example.com`.

  Example: `wesbos.bsky.social`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.emoji" type="string">
  Any single emoji to represent you in the directory. Can be left as an empty string.

  Example: `🚀`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.computer" type="string">
  The type of computer you primarily use. Accepted values:

  - `apple`
  - `windows`
  - `linux`
  - `bsd`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.phone" type="string">
  The type of phone you use. Accepted values:

  - `iphone`
  - `android`
  - `windowsphone`
  - `flipphone`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="body.tags" type="string[]">
  An array of strings describing your tools, technologies, or areas of interest. Used for filtering in the directory. There is no fixed list — use whatever describes you best.

  <Tip>
    Include a mix of languages, frameworks, editors, and interests. The more relevant tags you add, the easier it is for people to find your page when filtering.
  </Tip>
</ParamField>
